# Fidel Telegram — Launch Video Plan (v2)

## Concept
A retro-terminal CTF launch video: monospace, amber-on-dark, glitchy text, typewriter effects, matrix-style fidel rain. Feels like a hacker terminal from 1896 meeting 1980s cyberpunk.

## Audience
CTF players, crypto enthusiasts, hackers.

## Visual identity
- Background: near-black (#0a0a0a) with subtle grid
- Primary text: amber (#ffb000) monospace
- Accent: crimson (#b22222) for alerts/flag
- Fidel script: rendered in terracotta over the amber
- Scanlines, cursor blink, glitch flashes
- 1920×1080, 30fps, ~20s

## Storyboard (20s)

| Scene | Duration | Content |
|-------|----------|---------|
| 1 Boot | 0–3s | Terminal boot sequence: "ADWA-CTF TERMINAL v1.0", loading fidel module, glitch effects |
| 2 Hook | 3–6s | Fidel rain: characters fall like matrix, coalesce into "ጃዛጤዝሬ..." with "UNKNOWN CIPHER DETECTED" |
| 3 Reveal | 6–10s | Decryption in progress: affine + Vigenère bars filling, flag types out character by character |
| 4 Crib | 10–14s | Known-plaintext attack: "TELEGRAM" crib highlighted, key derivation shown |
| 5 Solve | 14–18s | solve.py runs, flag fully revealed in crimson, "FLAG CAPTURED" |
| 6 Outro | 18–20s | "AdwaCTF — Fidel Telegram — 100 pts — Break the cipher" |

## Tone
Deadpan, hacker-terminal, serious but playful.

## Share copy
Break a 1896 Ethiopian telegram cipher in Ge'ez fidel. Affine + Vigenère, crib "TELEGRAM". AdwaCTF crypto — can you crack it?
